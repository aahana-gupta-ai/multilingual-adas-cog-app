Study Title: Culturally Adapted ADAS-Cog Validation in Indian Clinical Settings

Principal Investigator: Aahana Gupta, Cardiff Sixth Form College
Scientific Advisor: Dr. Praveen Gupta, Marengo Asia Hospital, Gurugram

Purpose: This study aims to validate a culturally and linguistically adapted version of the Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale–Cognitive Subscale (ADAS-Cog) for use with Indian patients. Your participation helps improve cognitive assessment tools for Indian populations.

What will happen: The patient will complete 11 cognitive tasks administered by their neurologist using this application. The session takes approximately 30–45 minutes.

Data collected: Patient demographics (age, education, city), cognitive scores, language used, and word list administered. No personally identifying information beyond name is stored. All data is encrypted and stored securely.

Voluntary participation: Participation is entirely voluntary. The patient or caregiver may withdraw at any time without affecting their clinical care.

Data use: Anonymous data will be used solely for academic research and publication. No commercial use without explicit consent.

Contact: For questions about this research, contact Dr. Praveen Gupta, Marengo Asia Hospital, Gurugram — Phone: 098186 87903.
I confirm that the patient and/or their caregiver has been informed about this study, understands its purpose, and consents to participate voluntarily.
I confirm that consent has been obtained and will be documented in the patient's clinical record, in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki.
